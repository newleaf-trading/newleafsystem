The two legibility laws

Law 1 · Playfair floor
Playfair 900 ≥ 40px

Big and heavy: the Didone contrast reads clean.

Below 40px → Inter takes over (like this line).
Never: small Playfair / gold text
Thin Playfair at 13px turns to grey mush once the feed compresses it.
Gold on cream ≈ 2:1 — illegible

Law 2: gold is a surface (pill fill), never text on light. Text on cream is forest (~13.7:1).

Both laws are encoded as constants in brand-tokens.js (minDisplaySize:40, thumbHeadlineMin:64, goldIsSurfaceOnly:true) and enforced by a golden-frame regression — not left to per-comp judgement.

04 · Thumbnail system

Three thumbnail variants per video

Prop-driven Remotion stills off the same tokens. Playfair 900 headline (≥64px), gold kicker pill with forest text, ticker chip, verdict badge, logo bug. We render three angles and pick the winner — thumbnail choice moves CTR more than anything else here.

05 · Safe areas & captions

Numbers, not “clear the UI”

Vertical platforms overlay their own chrome. Content sits inside a <SafeArea> with pinned reserves so nothing is guessed — and Reels ship with burned-in captions above the caption reserve, because Reels autoplay muted and captions are the top completion-rate lever.

08 · Needs you, not code

Four decisions to unblock Phase 4

None block Phases 1–3. Publishing is free (API quotas are rate limits, not dollars) — the only real cost in the pipeline is clip generation, unchanged by this work.

1 · YouTube audit gate
Is the API project already audited so uploads honor privacyStatus? If not, uploads force to private → Phase 4 YT is thumbnail-only until the audit clears.
2 · Instagram account
Confirm a Business/Creator account linked to a Facebook Page (required by the Graph API). Start the linkage now — it’s the long pole.
3 · R2 exposure for IG
Graph API fetches the master from R2 over minutes — public public/social/ prefix, or presign ≥1h? A small security-posture call.
4 · YouTube OAuth
Do we have client credentials for the channel, or ship thumbnails-only for YT until we do?
