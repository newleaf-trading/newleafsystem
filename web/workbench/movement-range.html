How a setup is scored

Every symbol goes through two stages. Stage 1 always drives the ranking; Stage 2 is an optional trend filter you switch on with Trend filter.

Stage 1 · Setup Quality (0–100) — always live
A deterministic reaction-engine score: how good is this level to sell premium against, right now? Seven weighted components:
Zone score ×0.30 — how reliably price holds/rejects the zone you'd sell against (Wilson-smoothed rate, needs ≥3 touches).
Premium richness ×0.20 — are you paid enough? Implied vs realized vol (IV/RV).
Regime confidence ×0.15 — how clearly range-bound vs trending.
Distance / approach ×0.15 — how close price is to the zone, and how fast it's approaching.
Option liquidity ×0.10 — tradeable spreads & open interest.
Forward-return edge ×0.10 — historical median 5-day move from similar touches.
Event-risk penalty − — subtracts for earnings / ex-div inside the trade window.
Stage 2 · Minervini Trend Template — optional overlay
A deterministic SEPA-style trend template (Mark Minervini) checks whether the prevailing trend backs or fights the short side: the 50/150/200-day MA stack, price vs that stack, 200-day slope, relative strength vs SPY, and 52-week structure. It emits one verdict:
ALIGNED uptrend backs the setup → Setup Quality + 5
CONFLICTED selling into a downtrend (falling knife) → Setup Quality × 0.6 — multiplicative, so the most tempting high-quality knives are cut hardest.
NEUTRAL no trend opinion → unchanged — unless a VCP base is tightening (imminent volatility expansion), then × 0.85.
Not yet proven. The multipliers (0.6 / +5 / 0.85) are placeholder starting points, not backtested — that's why the overlay is labelled UNVALIDATED. Leave the filter Off to rank on Stage 1 alone; switch it On to preview how a validated trend filter would re-rank.
Note: the verdict currently reads trend direction only — making it structure-aware (so a bearish setup in a downtrend counts as aligned) is a pending refinement.
The maths
Final = Stage 1, then (if the filter is on) × or + the Stage 2 adjustment, clamped 0–100. Example: a setup at 76 with a CONFLICTED verdict becomes 76 × 0.6 = 46.
