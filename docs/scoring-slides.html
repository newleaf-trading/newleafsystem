How we score to find trades

From 297 symbols to a handful of high-quality, trend-checked setups — deterministically.

Deterministic scoring · auditable · no black box2026
The job

Turn 297 symbols into a few setups worth a human's attention

Every symbol is scanned the same way. We don't show all of them — we score them, filter out the weak ones, and surface the top setups. Two questions decide a trade:

  • Is this a good level? — a support/resistance zone that has reliably held. → Setup Quality
  • Is the trend friendly? — is price trending into that level, or falling toward it? → Trend overlay
Key idea: a level that held 20 times still loses money if the stock is in a downtrend. Good scoring needs both answers.

Step 3 · The missing question

Setup Quality is trend-blind

It scores the level — never the direction the stock is travelling into it. So a great-looking support in a multi-month downtrend ranks at the top… as a falling knife.

The Trend overlay adds the missing question, deterministically — using the trend signals professionals already trust (Minervini, O'Neil, Weinstein).

The falling-knife problem

Rating a stair landing by how many people stopped there — without noticing the building is on fire and everyone's running down.

The Trend overlay · 6 checks → 1 verdict

Aligned · Neutral · Conflicted

Six checks from price & relative strength (MA stack 50>150>200, price above stack, 200-day rising, RS vs SPY, 52-week position). Asymmetric on purpose: slow to endorse, quick to flag.

Aligned
All four "up" checks pass — the trend backs the short side.
endorse · SQ + 5
Neutral

unchanged · ×0.85 if VCP

demote · SQ × 0.6

Worked example

TLT — the engine loved it, the trend vetoes it

  • Setup Quality 79 — a strong $87 support zone, 74% smoothed hold-rate, rich premium (IV/RV 1.30).
  • But the trend is down: MA stack inverted (50<150<200), price below the 200-day, 200-day rolling over → 3 of 4 down-checks fire.
  • Verdict: Conflicted → 79 × 0.6 = would become 47.
Why this matters

Sorted by Setup Quality, TLT sits at the top of "tradeable." Without the trend check, you'd sell puts into a downtrend. The overlay surfaces exactly that risk — before you trade it.
